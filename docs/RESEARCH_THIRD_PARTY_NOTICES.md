# Research Studio dependencies

The six CSL style files in frontend/public/research-csl are unmodified copies from the open CSL styles collection distributed by citeproc-py-styles. Their embedded metadata identifies authors and licenses (Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0). Preserve those metadata and notices when distributing styles. Source: https://github.com/citation-style-language/styles ; license: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/ .

Tiptap, Citation.js and KaTeX are used under their own open-source licenses. Noto fonts retain their SIL Open Font License notices in installed distributions. Pandoc, TeX, scientific Python and Docker images have their respective licenses. Publisher layout presets are independently defined formatting settings, not certified official publisher templates.
